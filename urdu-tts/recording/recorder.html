  • Quiet room; fan and AC off. Same microphone and distance (20–30 cm) every session.
  • After pressing Space, wait for “Speak now” (half a second of silence is recorded first, to measure the room), speak, pause briefly, then press Space.
  • Talk naturally, like on a friendly call with a customer. Not too fast, not like the news.
  • A mistake or a stumble? Just press R and say it again: the new take replaces the old one.
  • Take a break every 30–40 minutes; a tired voice sounds different.
